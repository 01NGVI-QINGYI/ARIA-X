# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

- Be direct, patient and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep discussions on the project's scope and documented boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior, spam or bad-faith engagement.
- Sharing credentials, private target data or exploit details outside the agreed disclosure process.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, reject contributions or restrict
participation when this policy is violated. For sensitive reports, use GitHub's reporting tools or
the private security advisory flow rather than a public thread.
